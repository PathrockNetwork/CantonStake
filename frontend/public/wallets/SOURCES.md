# Wallet icon sources

Bundled logos identify compatible wallet providers. Brand trademarks belong to their respective owners.

RainbowKit SVGs are used under its MIT license (LICENSE-rainbowkit). Cosmos Wallet Registry SVGs are used under CC BY 4.0 (LICENSE-cosmos-wallet-registry). Polkadot.js logo is sourced from Talisman Connect. Slush and Petra icons come from the installed wallet SDKs; both SDKs declare Apache-2.0 (license text in LICENSE-aptos).

Assets are unchanged; render sizing is handled by CSS.
